IT Security Policy

1. Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
- Changed every 90 days
- No password reuse for last 5 passwords
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) mandatory for all systems

2. Device Security
- Company devices only for work purposes
- Full disk encryption required
- Automatic screen lock after 5 minutes
- Anti-virus software must be kept updated
- Personal devices require MDM enrollment for company email

3. Data Protection
- Sensitive data must be encrypted
- No storing company data on personal cloud storage
- USB drives must be encrypted and approved
- Email encryption for confidential information

4. Access Control
- Principle of least privilege
- Access reviews quarterly
- Immediate revocation upon termination
- Guest access limited to 24 hours

5. Incident Reporting
- Security incidents must be reported within 1 hour
- Contact IT security team immediately
- Do not attempt to resolve security breaches yourself
- Phishing emails must be reported and deleted
