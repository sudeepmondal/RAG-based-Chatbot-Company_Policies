Remote Work Policy

1. Eligibility
- Employees must have completed 6 months probation
- Job role must be suitable for remote work
- Manager approval required
- Performance reviews must be satisfactory

2. Remote Work Options
- Full remote: 5 days per week from home
- Hybrid: 2-3 days remote, 2-3 days office
- Occasional remote: As needed with prior approval

3. Requirements
- Reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps)
- Dedicated workspace
- Available during core hours (10 AM - 4 PM)
- Response time: Within 2 hours during work hours

4. Equipment
- Company laptop provided
- Monitor and accessories: $500 allowance
- Internet reimbursement: $50 per month
- Home office setup: One-time $300 allowance

5. Communication
- Daily standup meetings via video call
- Weekly team sync required
- Slack/Teams must be active during work hours
- Camera on for meetings unless technical issues
