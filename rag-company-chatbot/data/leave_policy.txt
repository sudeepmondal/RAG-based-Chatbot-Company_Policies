Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
- All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year
- Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis
- Leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance
- Maximum 10 consecutive days can be taken at once
- Unused leave can be carried over up to 5 days to the next year

2. Sick Leave
- Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually
- Medical certificate required for absence exceeding 2 consecutive days
- Sick leave cannot be carried over to the next year

3. Emergency Leave
- Up to 3 days per year for family emergencies
- Must be reported within 24 hours
- Manager approval required

4. Parental Leave
- Maternity leave: 16 weeks paid leave
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks paid leave
- Adoption leave: 8 weeks paid leave
